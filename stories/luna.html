Cat

Luna

She showed up on the back porch during a rainstorm and never left.

6 Years Together
1 Person

She arrived without ceremony. A rainstorm on a Tuesday evening, the kind that turns the air heavy and makes the porch boards slick. She was just there, wet and silent, watching through the screen door. The next morning she was still there. By the third day, the back porch was simply where she lived.

The routine came quickly. Every morning she sat on the exact same windowsill and watched the street. Cars, joggers, delivery trucks — she tracked them all with the same unblinking attention. Every evening she slept on the same corner of the couch, curled into herself, her tail tucked in. She moved through the house like she was following a script only she could read.

She hated everyone except one person. That person she followed everywhere.

It wasn't affection in the usual sense. She didn't purr on command or come when called. But she was always in the same room, always within sight. Kitchen, office, bedroom — wherever that one person went, Luna followed. If they sat down, she appeared. If they stood, she watched. It was companionship without performance, loyalty without fanfare.

The one time she climbed onto a stranger's lap and fell asleep, everyone stopped talking. It was a Tuesday afternoon, someone visiting for coffee, nothing special. Luna walked across the room, jumped up, circled twice, and closed her eyes. Ten minutes later she woke up, looked around as if surprised to find herself there, and left. It never happened again.

The silent companion who chose one person and meant it. She came in from the rain and stayed for six years, and in that time she taught everyone around her that loyalty doesn't need to be loud to be absolute.
